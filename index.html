Partnership program

Regular people. Real vehicles. Income from the network.

CAB AI is not assembling a corporate fleet. It is the application layer for individuals and small operators who purchase a robotaxi and want that asset working. List the vehicle, accept rides through the app, and earn from trips the platform sends your way — the same logic as a ride network that works with people who already own the car.

Owners keep the vehicle. CAB AI supplies demand, Live View, trip records, and rider support tools.

Signature software

Live View — built as a privacy feature, not a public feed

CAB AI Live View is designed as one-of-a-kind trip software: a private window into the vehicle so riders and partners can confirm the unit is in order — route, cabin readiness, and approach — without putting footage on someone else’s server by default.

$2.99

Live View subscription

Riders unlock Live View as an optional add-on planned at $2.99 per trip (or equivalent plan). Payment is what keeps the stream private to that customer — not a free broadcast.

REC

Recording + auto-save

Live View includes video recording. When the trip ends, the file saves automatically to the rider’s phone for evidence, insurance, or personal security. Their tape. Their device.

OK

Status at a glance

Partners and riders can check that the vehicle is operating as expected — charge, ETA, cabin, and inbound path — before and during the ride.

In-ride support

Two ways to get help while the car is moving

If something feels off, the rider should not be stuck with a dead chat bot. CAB AI plans two support paths from inside the trip.

Planned product

Key features

What the application is built to do

01

Vehicle intel

Battery, range, seats, model, charge, ETA, and fare — before confirm. Partners see the same health data on their unit.

02

Trip music

Pick a playlist while the robotaxi is inbound so cabin audio is ready when the door opens.

03

Live View + save

$2.99 private stream, on-trip recording, automatic save to the rider’s phone.

04

Dual support

CAB AI Support for the platform. Extensive Support for a live conversation with the owner.
