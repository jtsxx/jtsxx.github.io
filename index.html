CAB AI is the network layer between riders and independently owned autonomous vehicles. Book with full vehicle intel. Watch the unit approach. Keep the trip on your phone.

Explore the experience How the network works
Operator network

Ownership stays with the partner. Demand runs through the app.

CAB AI does not purchase a corporate fleet. Independent owners — individuals and small operators — connect eligible robotaxis to the platform. Rides are matched through the application. Yield from those trips belongs to the person who holds the vehicle.

Think of established ride networks: the software coordinates the market. The asset remains private.

Vision — not live yet

Designed for owners

If the vehicle is yours, the network is how it works. Listing, dispatch, Live View health, and rider tools — without surrendering the car.

Live View

See the vehicle. Keep the tape. On your device.

Live View is a private window into the inbound unit — path, cabin readiness, charge, ETA — so riders and partners can confirm the trip is in order. It is not a public camera feed.

$2.99

Private access

Planned at $2.99 per trip. The fee is how the stream stays exclusive to that rider instead of sitting on an open server.

REC

Recorded to the phone

Every Live View session records. At drop-off the file saves automatically to the rider’s phone — evidence, insurance, or peace of mind.

OK

Operational clarity

Charge, route, cabin, and inbound path in one frame. Confirm the unit before you step in.

In-ride support

Help that can reach the desk — or the owner.

Riders should not be left with a dead loop when something feels wrong. Two lines ship inside the trip.
