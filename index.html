CAB AI is built like a modern taxi network. Riders request a robotaxi. People who already have a vehicle apply to become partners and take those trips on the platform.

For riders

Request a robotaxi. Know what is coming.

Set pickup and destination. Review the vehicle — model, seats, charge, ETA, fare — then confirm. Add music for the cabin. Open Live View if you want eyes on the trip. Ask for help from CAB AI, or from the partner behind that vehicle.

Partner program

Have a robotaxi? Apply to drive it on CAB AI.

This is the same structure riders already understand from Uber: the app finds the trip. The partner provides the vehicle. CAB AI does not buy the cars. Eligible owners apply, get approved, and list their robotaxi on the network.

Partners keep the vehicle. The application handles requests, routing, Live View for riders, and payouts when the service is live.

Applications not open yet

Who can apply

Individuals and small operators who own an eligible autonomous vehicle and want it on a ride network — subject to local rules, insurance, and approval.

Optional extra

Live View — extra security, if you want it.

Live View is not included in every fare. It is an optional subscription add-on on each trip for riders who want to watch the vehicle, record the ride, and keep that file on their phone.

$2.99

Per-trip subscription

Add Live View for a planned $2.99 on that ride. Skip it and the trip still books like a normal taxi request.

REC

Record + save

The stream records. When the trip ends, the video saves automatically to the rider’s phone — for security and personal records.

OK

Watch the approach

See the inbound vehicle, charge, route, and ETA so you know the unit is in order before you get in.
