You buy the robotaxi.
We build the app that puts it to work.

CAB AI is not buying a company fleet. We are building the booking, dispatch, live-view, and trip layer for people who own robotaxis — the same idea as Uber hiring drivers who already own their cars.

Partnership program

Owners bring the cars. CAB AI brings the rides.

When robotaxis are legal to operate in a market, anyone who buys or already owns an eligible autonomous vehicle can apply to list it on CAB AI. You keep the asset. The app matches passengers, shows live hood-cam and ETA, saves the trip on the rider’s phone, and routes payouts to the owner — not a giant in-house fleet sitting on our balance sheet.

Think Uber’s original model: the platform does not purchase every car. People who already invested in the vehicle plug into demand.

Who it’s for

Early owners, small operators, and future retail buyers of robotaxis who want software, riders, and trip records — without handing the car to a central fleet buyer.

Product promise

Mobility that feels premium — and transparent

No black-box dispatch. When a rider orders, they see the partner vehicle: battery, model, seats, charge, ETA. They choose the soundtrack before wheels turn. They can watch the unit approach in view mode. The recording lives on their device, not a mystery server.

Key features

What riders and partners unlock

01

Vehicle intel

Battery %, range, seats, model, charge status, ETA, and fare estimate — before the rider confirms. Owners see the same facts on their unit.

02

Trip music

Riders pick a playlist while the partner robotaxi is inbound so cabin audio is ready the moment they step in.

03

Live view mode

Watch the partner unit head toward you — camera-style framing plus map context. Trip tape stays on the phone.
