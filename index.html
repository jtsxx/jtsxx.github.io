CAB AI

CAB AI is a taxi app for robotaxis. You request a ride the same way you would in any cab app. If you own a robotaxi, you can apply to become a partner and take trips on CAB AI. Live View is optional — $2.99 extra on a trip if you want to watch the car and keep a recording on your phone.

See the app Partner program
Rides

Book a robotaxi. See the car first.

Enter pickup and drop-off. Check the vehicle, seats, charge, arrival time, and price. Confirm the ride. Add music if you want. Add Live View if you want extra security. If you need help, you can reach CAB AI or the partner who owns that car.

Partners

Own a robotaxi? Apply to join CAB AI.

Same idea as Uber: the app sends the trip, you bring the vehicle. CAB AI does not buy the cars. You apply, get approved, and your robotaxi can take rides on the app.

Applications not open yet

Who it’s for

People and small operators who already have (or will have) a robotaxi and want it on CAB AI, subject to local rules and insurance.

Live View

Extra security, only if you add it.

A normal ride does not include Live View. If you want to watch the car come to you and keep a video on your phone, add it for that trip.
