Clarity before hype

The vision is ambitious.
The claims should stay honest.

A community-powered project that starts with a coin and aims, stage by stage, at building the groundwork for an autonomous transportation network. The token is the spark. The network is the goal.
No. CAB AI is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Tesla.
Tesla is the most visible public conversation around robotaxis. CAB AI uses that conversation as context for a community project—not as a claim of partnership, access, or official involvement.
It begins as a community token. The point of the project is to refuse to stop there. Every public claim should be tied to work that can be shown: product, legal review, operations, and transparent progress.
A hypothetical concept for how a future transportation business might allocate a portion of revenue toward the community if—and only if—a real business exists. It is not a dividend, not a promise, and not a prediction.
Any future qualification rules would be published clearly before they matter. Nothing on this site creates a right to revenue, tokens, or special treatment.
Only if technology, regulation, insurance, capital, and operations all line up. The honest path is to build the app, the community, and the legal foundation first—then attempt operations when those constraints allow.
Today: a simulated ride experience so people can feel the product vision. Later: booking, fleet status, and network tools if the project earns the right to operate.
Routing, dispatch, support, and simulation first. Autonomy in the real world is a regulated, capital-heavy problem—not a slogan.
Early holders are the community that funds attention, design, and development. That does not automatically entitle anyone to future revenue. Loyalty should be recognized through transparent rules if a real business ever exists.
