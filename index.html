Product promise

Mobility that feels premium — and transparent

No black-box dispatch. When you order, you see the machine: battery, model, seats, charge, ETA. You choose the soundtrack before wheels turn. You can watch the vehicle approach in view mode.

Key features

What you’ll unlock in the app

01

Vehicle intel

Battery %, range, seats, model, charge status, ETA, and fare estimate — before you confirm the ride.

02

Trip music

Pick a playlist while the robotaxi is inbound so cabin audio is ready the moment you step in.

03

Live view mode

Watch your unit head toward you — camera-style framing plus map context so you always know where it is.
