The ride, in full view.

CAB AI is a premium autonomous ride application. See the vehicle before it arrives. Choose the cabin. Keep a private record of the trip on your phone.

Explore the experience Live View
The product

Clarity before the doors open.

No black-box dispatch. Before you confirm, you see battery, range, seats, model, charge, ETA, and fare. On the way, you can open a private Live View of the inbound vehicle. After the trip, the recording stays on your device.

Concept stage

This site is the product direction. There is no live dispatch today — only a walkthrough of how CAB AI is meant to feel.

Live View

A private window into the trip.

Live View lets you watch the vehicle approach and confirm the unit is in order — route, cabin, charge, ETA. It is designed as a paid, private stream. Not a public camera.

$2.99

Private access

Planned at $2.99 per trip. The fee keeps the stream exclusive to that rider.

REC

Saved to your phone

The session records. At the end of the ride the file saves automatically to the rider’s phone — for security or personal records.

OK

Status at a glance

Charge, path, cabin, and arrival time in one view before you step in.

In-ride support

Two lines. One from the app. One from the operator.

If something feels wrong mid-trip, help should not dead-end in a script.

Planned product
